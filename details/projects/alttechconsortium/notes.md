## What it is
AltTech operates in sustainable building management / green technology, helping commercial properties cut operating costs while improving environmental performance. Their pitch frames sustainability as a financial gain ("every dollar saved is 3x of top line") rather than a cost, targeting sectors like hospitality, healthcare, real estate, pharma, chemical, FMCG, civil engineering, automotive, and food & beverage. They position customers as "prosumers" who both produce and consume resources.
