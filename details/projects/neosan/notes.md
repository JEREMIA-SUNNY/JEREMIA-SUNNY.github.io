## What it is
Neo San is a waste-management technology company focused on "reject waste" — non-recyclable material that conventional recycling can't process. Their flagship product, the Neo-X incinerator family, processes waste on-site at temperatures above 1200°C, claiming 98% cleaner emissions than alternative disposal methods, and reports real-time emissions data for compliance.

## Site sections / key features observed
- Nav: Technology, About, Impact, Contact, Careers
- Target segments called out: corporates/MRFs/airports/industry, institutions (schools, hospitals), residential complexes, government/municipalities, and specialized needs like biomedical/menstrual hygiene waste
- Headline impact metrics: 235+ tonnes of waste handled, 500+ tonnes CO₂ offset, 200+ units deployed across 8 cities
- Two deployment models: outright purchase or managed-service partnership
- Named partnerships: World Economic Forum, NITI Aayog, IIM Bangalore
- Cost-savings claim: "Rs 10+ savings per kilogram" of waste processed
