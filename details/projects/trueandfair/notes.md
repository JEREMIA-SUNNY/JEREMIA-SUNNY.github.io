## What it is
A financial auditing and regulatory-compliance organization positioned as a standard-setter for audit quality in India, addressing fragmented audit practices and expectations from regulators like NFRA. It serves auditing firms, corporates needing audit/governance advisory, regulators, and Chartered Accountants seeking training.

## Site sections / key features observed
- ADAT® (Audit Documentation & Archival Tool) — documentation platform with work-paper templates and risk libraries
- ADOC — content library supporting financial statement audits
- In-Tuition training program (classroom, virtual, hybrid)
- Quality Leadership advisory programs for audit methodology/policy
- Nav includes services, training resources, a regulatory library, videos, blogs, and discussion forums
