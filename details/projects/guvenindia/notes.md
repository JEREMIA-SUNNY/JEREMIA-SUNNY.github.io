## Site sections / key features observed

- Featured Products showcase linking to the full catalog

- New Arrivals section
- Customer testimonials ("Words From Our Standard")
- Hero banner carrying the core brand message
- Standard e-commerce plumbing: account management, legal pages (Privacy Policy, Terms of Service), contact details (Mumbai HQ, email, phone)
