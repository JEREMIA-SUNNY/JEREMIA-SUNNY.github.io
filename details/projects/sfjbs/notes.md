## What it is
SFJ Business Solutions is an enterprise workforce transformation and skilling partner based in Bengaluru, India. It runs corporate upskilling (Knowledge-as-a-Service in AI, cloud, and emerging tech), institutional training for universities, government skilling missions, and CSR-focused employability programs, alongside software platforms — an AI-enabled LMS, "Talent OS" for talent acquisition/workforce management, and "CASPA," an AI-powered ERP for SMEs — plus staffing (Talent-as-a-Service). The homepage claims 500+ enterprise clients, 15 years in the market, presence in 25+ countries, and 300,000+ professionals trained, serving Fortune 500 enterprises, educational institutions, government agencies, and CSR programs across 29+ industry verticals.
